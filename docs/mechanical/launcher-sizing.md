**Getting this rate from stock springs.** Rate scales with 1 / active length: measure your spring's rate (hang a known mass, read the deflection), then cut it to `L_cut = L_measured × k_measured / k_needed`. Its free length must exceed 30 mm + solid length (coils × wire diameter) + 3 mm; if it doesn't, use two softer springs in parallel on the two guide rods.
