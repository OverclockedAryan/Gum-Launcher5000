# M2 bench rig: launcher, feed and chronograph

Deliverable 3, part one. This is a fixed test stand that proves the two riskiest pieces before any turret exists: **exit-speed scatter ≤ 1 %** with real gum (REQ-PERF-004), and **zero double feeds in 200 cycles** (REQ-FEED-002). It is built from the same modules the robot's head will use, so whatever passes here moves straight into M4.

- **Numbers:** every dimension below comes from [launcher-sizing.md](launcher-sizing.md), generated by `tools/launcher_design.py`.
- **CAD:** the printable parts are generated by `cad/bench_rig.py` (build123d) from the same numbers, with interference checks; see [bench-rig-cad.md](bench-rig-cad.md).
- **Placeholders:** until you measure pellets, the geometry assumes a 17 × 11 × 7 mm, 1.45 g pellet. After measuring, re-run the tool with your numbers before printing anything that touches the pellet:

```powershell
python tools/launcher_design.py --mass-g 1.42 --length-mm 16.8 --width-mm 10.9 --thick-mm 6.9 --thick-min-mm 6.6 --thick-max-mm 7.2 --width-max-mm 11.1 --length-max-mm 17.2
.venv-cad\Scripts\python cad/bench_rig.py   # same flags; regenerates the STLs
```

## Layout (side view)

```text
                 magazine tube (12 pellets on the rig, 24 on the robot)
                       ┌───┐
                       │ ▭ │
                       │ ▭ │
                       │ ▭ │   shuttle slides in/out of the page (14.7 mm)
  springs on rods      │ ▭ │
  ┌─preload screw      └─┬─┘
  │  ╱╲╱╲╱╲╱╲ ┌──────┐  ┌▼──────┐┌──────────────────┐┌────────────────┐
  ├──╱╲╱╲╱╲╱╲─┤ sled ├──┤breech │ channel  11.7×7.9  ││ chrono  60 mm  │──► pellet
  │  ═══ 3 mm guide rods ══════════════════════════════│ gate1     gate2│
  │           └──┬───┘ bumper (TPU)                    └────────────────┘
  │              │ follower arm to the side (623ZZ roller, at rod height)
  │              ▼
  │           ( snail cam ⌀70 ) ◄── JGY370 worm motor (beside the sled bay)
  └─────────── cradle, pivots at the muzzle end, elevation 0–35° ─────────
```

**How it moves.** The cam turns in a vertical plane beside the sled (the −Y side), with its centre at the rod height, so the cam's push lines up with the springs in height. The cam sits *ahead of* the follower. As its radius grows, it pushes the follower and sled **backward** 22 mm against the springs. At the drop, the springs drive the sled forward. The sled pushes the pellet, which sits against its face from the start (D-001: push, never hit), then the sled stops on the TPU bumper. The follower comes to rest 1 mm above the cam's base circle, so it never strikes the cam (the cam lifts 23 mm: the 22 mm stroke plus that 1 mm gap).

## Modules and key dimensions

### 1. Sled and guides

| Feature | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Guide rods | 2 × 3 mm polished steel, **74.5 mm** long, 18 mm apart | Rod spacing clears the 11.7 mm channel plus its walls |
| Sled body | About 20 mm long, bores 3.2 mm (print at 3.2, ream if tight) | Sliding fit; horizontal holes get teardrop tops |
| Sled mass | **6.6 g** moving: 4.1 g printed + about 2.5 g of 623ZZ, axle screw and nut (weigh it, then re-run the tool with `--sled-g`) | ≈ 4.5× the pellet: ±10 % pellet mass gives only ∓0.8 % speed. The CAD showed the original 4.5 g target can't carry a 623ZZ follower |
| Push face | 11.3 × 7.5 mm, flat, in the channel | Pushes the pellet's end face; same section as the channel minus clearance |
| Springs | **Two** in parallel, one over each rod, together 0.469 N/mm (0.235 N/mm each), compressed 8 → 30 mm | The rods stop the springs buckling. Robu's 0.5 mm × OD 6 mm × 40 mm springs (about 0.2 N/mm each, estimate) are the first candidate, the 0.6 mm wire spring the second: measure them |
| Spring free length | ≥ 32.5 mm + solid length + 1 mm | At the hard stop a 40 mm spring is squeezed to 7.5 mm; about 13 coils of 0.5 mm wire are 6.5 mm solid, which leaves 1 mm. Check yours |
| Preload screw | M3 × 25 through an insert in the rear wall, pushing a bar that seats both springs. **Hard stop at +2.5 mm**: the bar's tab hits the front wall of a pocket printed in the floor | Trims speed from 4.6 to 6.4 m/s; the stop keeps the worst case at 35 mJ, under the 40 mJ cap (REQ-SAF-003) |
| Follower | 623ZZ on an M3 × 10 screw, on an arm off the sled's −Y side, at rod height | Roller follower: low friction, pressure angle ≤ 30°. The 19 mm sideways offset loads the rods only while cocking, never during the shot |
| Bumper | TPU 95A pad at the sled's forward stop | Absorbs about 131 mJ per shot; replaceable |

### 2. Snail cam and motor

| Feature | Value |
|---|---|
| Profile | Generated into `cad/bench_rig/stl/cam.stl`. Also in `cad/launcher/cam_profile.dxf` (layer CAM is the outline, PITCH the roller-centre path) |
| Size | Base circle 12 mm radius, peak 35 mm radius (⌀70 mm cam), 5 mm thick, ⌀14 hub |
| Angles | 280° rise (constant torque), 20° full-lift dwell, radial drop, 60° landing |
| Shaft | JGY370 6 mm D-shaft: D-bore plus an M3 set screw in a heat-set insert onto the flat. Turns **clockwise** seen from the motor to cock |
| Torque | 0.52 kg·cm peak against 2.0 kg·cm rated: ×3.8 margin |
| Timing | Cock 0.67 s, decock 0.67 s (reverse), fire latency 24 ms from the park point |
| "Ready" switch | KW4 microswitch tripped by a flag on the cam hub at the **middle of the dwell**, on slotted holes for adjustment. Parking there holds the cocked spring with zero torque |
| Mounting | Gearbox held by two zip ties in a printed cradle (the JGY370 hole pattern isn't published); the cam centre sits on the follower's line of travel, so the force stays radial |

### 3. Channel and breech

- **Channel:** 11.7 × 7.9 mm: the largest pellet plus 0.4 mm (pellet flat, long axis along the barrel). Roll-lock margin 1.1 mm, so the pellet can't turn over.
- **Printing it:** the launcher body (U-section, sled bay and feed housing in one piece, so the channel and the rods stay aligned) and a lid whose rib forms the channel ceiling. Both are printed with the sliding faces up, with ironing.
- **Breech:** a 17.9 mm-long window where the shuttle brings the pellet in from the side, **exactly at the face of the cocked sled**. A 45° lead-in steps the ceiling down from the breech to the channel.
- **Exit guide:** about 30 mm of channel after the sled's forward stop, then the chronograph sleeve.

### 4. Shuttle and magazine

| Feature | Value |
|---|---|
| Shuttle | A T-section slider: two side walls **8.25 mm** tall form a 17.9 × 11.7 mm pocket (1.1 × the thickest pellet; less than two of the thinnest at 13.0 mm, so only one can ever fit). The pocket is open front and back, so the tongue and pellet pass straight through; a 2 mm ring above bridges the walls and runs in the tunnel's upper level. The tunnel walls hold the pellet in X while it moves |
| Travel | 14.7 mm, from under the stack to the breech; the tunnel's closed end is the load stop |
| Drive | MG90S under a shelf on the feed housing. A 10.2 mm horn sweeping 92° drives an M2 pin in a slot in the shuttle tab (scotch yoke) |
| Return | An extension spring pulls the shuttle back under the stack, so the breech is empty with power off (REQ-SAF-015). Test T7 checks whether it really back-drives the unpowered MG90S; if not, switch to a JF-0530B spring-return solenoid with a 1.5:1 lever |
| Interlock | The shuttle moves only while the "ready" switch is closed, because the sled passes through the window when it fires. After a shot the tongue sits in the window, so the sequence is always COCK, then LOAD or UNLOAD |
| Magazine bore | 18.1 × 11.9 mm, pellets stacked flat; clear PETG with a viewing slot |
| Height | 12 pellets on the rig (≈ 90 mm + lid). On the robot, 24 pellets need 180 mm + 25 mm for the follower and spring |

### 5. Chronograph sleeve

- **Geometry:** a 70 mm sleeve, 1 mm larger than the channel so the pellet doesn't touch it. Two gates **60 mm** apart, each an IR LED facing a photodiode through **1.5 mm slits**; the slits set the beam width and give sharp edges.
- **Electronics:** photodiode → LM393 comparator → microcontroller pin. Timestamp the edges with PIO on a Pico or the capture peripheral on an ESP32. 1 µs timing adds 0.01 % error; 50 µs MicroPython interrupt jitter would add 0.5 %, too much for a 1 % SD test.
- **Spacing:** the real error term (±0.2 mm is ±0.33 %). Measure the printed block with the caliper, and put the measured value in the firmware config.
- **Material:** print the sleeve in **black PETG**: it blocks stray IR, which light colours don't.

### 6. Cradle and catch box

The launcher module pivots on an M5 bolt through a boss under the muzzle end, so the muzzle height barely changes with elevation. At the rear, a lug on the body carries an M5 bolt and wing nut in a 122 mm-radius arc slot on the stand's quadrant, with tick marks every 5°. Together they set the elevation from 0° to 35°, enough for the seed-table elevations at 0.5–2 m ([ballistics](../research/ballistics.md) §7). Fire into a cardboard box lined with a towel, with a paper target on the back wall for the drop tests.

| Channel halves, shuttle, breech, magazine | Natural PETG, **stainless nozzle** (food path, REQ-FEED-004) | 0.12 mm | 3 walls, 30 % gyroid | Channel floor up, **ironing on**; reserve this spool for food parts |

| Cam | PETG, or ASA if it wears | 0.16 mm | 6 walls, 60 % gyroid | Flat on the bed: loads stay in-plane. Treat it as a wear part |
| Follower arm, spring seats, motor mount | PETG | 0.16 mm | 5 walls, 40 % | Loads along the layers, never across |
| Chronograph sleeve | **Black** PETG | 0.12 mm | 3 walls, 30 % | Slits vertical so they print cleanly |
| Bumper | TPU 95A | 0.2 mm | 100 % | Slow speed (about 30 mm/s) |
| Frame, cradle, catch-box fixtures | PETG or PLA+ | 0.2 mm | 3 walls, 20 % | PLA+ only for parts that see no spring load |

**Typical X1C settings:**

- **PETG:** 240–250 °C, bed 70 °C, fan 30–50 %, textured PEI plate.
- **ASA:** enclosure closed, 260 °C, bed 100 °C.
- **First print:** a 0.1–0.5 mm clearance coupon.
- **Fits:** 0.2–0.25 mm per side for sliding fits, about nominal for 623ZZ/608ZZ press fits (tune with a test ring), 4.0 mm holes for M3 heat-set inserts.

## Assembly order

1. Print the clearance coupon and the pellet gauge (a plate with 11.0–12.0 mm slots in 0.2 mm steps) to check your pellet spread against the channel.
2. **Sled on rods without springs.** It must slide freely under its own weight at 15° tilt; ream the bores until it does.
3. **Springs, preload screw and hard stop.** Measure the combined rate (test T1) before the cam goes on.
4. **Cam, motor and ready switch.** Dry-cycle without pellets (T2).
5. **Breech, shuttle and magazine.** Cycle-test the feed (T6) before any firing.
6. **Chronograph sleeve.** Calibrate it (T3), then fire.

## M2 test procedure

**Safety:** impact-rated glasses for everyone in the room, fire only into the catch box with nobody downrange, ARM switch off and spring decocked before touching the mechanism (REQ-SAF-016).

Log every test as CSV under `docs/measurements/`.

| Test | What | Pass criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| T0 Pellets | Weigh and caliper 30 pellets per brand (L, W, T, mass). Re-run `launcher_design.py` with the mean, minimum and maximum | Spread fits: pocket depth < 2 × thinnest, roll-lock margin > 0.5 mm | D-010 |
| T1 Spring rate | Hang known masses on the sled (rig vertical), read the deflection at 3 points | Within ±10 % of 0.469 N/mm; otherwise trim the springs and re-measure | D-001 |
| T2 Dry cycle | 50 cock/decock cycles, no pellet | Ready switch trips every time; cock ≤ 1 s; motor and cam cool | REQ-PERF-009 |
| T3 Chronograph | Calibrate the gate spacing with the caliper; cross-check 5 shots against 240 fps phone video over a ruler | Video and chronograph agree within 2 % | REQ-SAF-003 |
| T4 Energy cap | Preload screw at its hard stop, heaviest pellet (or a 1.6 g printed dummy), 10 shots | Every shot ≤ 40 mJ (for 1.6 g, ≤ 7.07 m/s) | REQ-SAF-003 |
| T5 Consistency | 3 strings of 30 shots at nominal preload, fresh pellets from one batch | SD ≤ 1.0 %, extreme spread ≤ 4 %, mean within ±3 % of 6.0 m/s | REQ-PERF-003, -004 |
| T6 Feed | 200 load/unload cycles from a full magazine; then 20 cycles each with the rig tilted to −15° and +45° | 0 double feeds, ≤ 1 misfeed; jams recover with reverse and retry | REQ-FEED-002, -003 |
| T7 Fail-closed | Cut power with the shuttle at the breech, 10 times | Breech empties every time (otherwise switch to the solenoid) | REQ-SAF-015 |
| T8 Drop | 10 shots each at 0.5, 1.0, 1.5 and 2.0 m at the seed-table elevation; measure the impact height on the target | Fit `--cd` in `ballistics_sim.py` and commit the new report | REQ-PERF-002 |
| T9 Coating | Inspect 20 fired pellets; check the channel for dust after 100 shots | No cracked shells; cleaning interval set | REQ-FEED-004 |

**If T5 fails**, the launcher research says under-powered springs scatter the most. Try the fixes in this order: stiffer spring with less preload; polish the rods and ream the sled; check the follower doesn't touch the cam after release; heavier sled; then the alternatives in D-001.
