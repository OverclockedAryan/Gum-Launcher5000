## Assumed and to measure

- **JGY370 gearbox:** modelled as 46 × 32 × 25 mm with the output shaft 34 mm from the motor end, centred in height; the motor can points to the rear. Sellers don't publish the hole pattern, so it's held by two zip ties against printed stops. Measure yours and re-run with `--gearbox-len-mm`, `--gearbox-height-mm`, `--gearbox-depth-mm`, `--gearbox-shaft-from-motor-end-mm`.
- **MG90S:** 22.8 × 12.2 mm body, tabs 32.5 mm, holes 27.8 mm apart, shaft 6.1 mm from one end. Shim under the tabs so the horn clears the shuttle tab by about 0.5 mm.
- **KW4 switch:** 20 × 10 × 6.4 mm, holes 9.5 mm apart. Its mounting holes are slotted ±1.5 mm: set it so the hub flag closes it at the park point. It sits 0.6 mm behind the cam's rear face, so keep the lever inside the hub zone.
- **Springs:** free length 40 mm assumed (`--spring-free-mm`).
- **Cam direction:** seen from the motor side, the cam turns **clockwise** to cock. If your motor's forward is the other way, swap its wires (don't mirror the cam: the flag and D-flat follow).
- **After a shot** the tongue sits in the breech window, so the shuttle can't return until the cam re-cocks. The firmware sequence is COCK, then LOAD or UNLOAD (bench-rig.md, interlock).
